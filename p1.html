How to Make a Simple Mandala

Follow these easy steps to create your own mandala.

  1. Take a plain sheet of paper.
  2. Draw a small circle at the centre.
  3. Draw light guide circles around the centre.
  4. Add dots, petals, curves, and geometric shapes.
  5. Repeat your patterns around the circle.
  6. Outline your design using a black pen.
  7. Add colours to make your mandala unique.

Tips for Beginners

  • Start with simple shapes.
  • Keep your patterns evenly spaced.
  • Use a pencil to plan your design.
  • Experiment with different patterns.
  • Be patient and enjoy the process.

Materials Required

Here are the materials you can use for mandala art.

  • Plain paper or a sketchbook
  • Pencil and eraser
  • Ruler
  • Compass
  • Black fine-tip pen
  • Colour pencils or sketch pens

Suggested Working Order

  1. Collect all the materials.
  2. Plan your mandala design.
  3. Draw the basic pattern.
  4. Add details and colours.
  5. Display your finished artwork.
